Profitability

Margins as % of revenue

Revenue is the base for all three margins. Gross and operating margins use the company’s own adjusted (non-GAAP) figures: LIGHT “adjusted gross margin” and adjusted income from operations, BATS “adjusted profit from operations”, PFE adjusted cost of sales and SI&A from the 10-K (PFE adjusted operating income is derived, †). BATS publishes no adjusted gross profit, so its gross margin is a labelled proxy. Net margin is reported profit attributable to shareholders. REITs and property companies: gross profit is the company’s own line (CTP) or a labelled proxy = total revenues − direct property costs (WPC); operating income is revenue − total operating expenses (WPC, with impairments and merger costs added back for the adjusted line) or the company’s “operating profit excl. valuation result” (CTP). The base is total revenues, which is wider than the reference portal’s “Revenues” line, so margins read lower than on the portal.

Dividend growth, year on year

Payout ratio = declared dividend per share (DPS × diluted shares) ÷ (operating cash flow − capex) (†), the same construction the reference portal uses; for companies that report interest in financing (BATS) the tooltip also shows the stricter after-interest figure. The 75% line is Lichtenfeld’s rule (payout on free cash flow below 75%), the same threshold as criterion 5 of your screening criteria, not a recommendation. Dividend per share is the dividend declared for each fiscal year. Streaks only count the years loaded in this dashboard.
